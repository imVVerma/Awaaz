## What you build on
### Models trained in India, for Indian speech

Gnani AI builds its own speech and language models and trains them on real telephonic audio, so they handle noisy calls, code-mixed speech and Indian languages natively. Deployed together on infrastructure the customer controls, they form **Artha**, the sovereign stack. You get to build on the same models.

* **Speech to text — Prisma v2.5**
  Trained on 14M+ hours of telephonic audio. Streams in under 200ms across 10+ Indian languages plus Hinglish, Tanglish and Benglish, according to Gnani AI's published benchmarks.
* **Text to speech (Beta) — Timbre v2.5**
  Speaks 10+ Indic languages with context-aware prosody, Indian names, numerals and lakh and crore cadence. Streams over WebSocket and SSE.
* **Reasoning (open weight) — Evon v3.3**
  A 30B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 128K context and 11 Indian languages natively. Apache 2.0 weights are available by request on Hugging Face.

## Eligibility
### Who can apply

The programme is open to students. Check all three conditions before you register:
* Currently enrolled in a degree or course at a college or university in India.
* 18 years or older on the day you register.
* Building solo, or with one teammate who also meets these conditions.

### Solo or a team of two
* **Solo:** You register, build and submit on your own.
* **Team of two:** You register and add your one teammate's email. Teams can't be larger than two. Teammates accept terms independently; contest communication goes to the primary registrant, and each of you is interviewed and considered for an offer individually.

---

## Read before you register
### The rules that matter most

1. **Offers and stipends are individual:** Teammates are interviewed separately, and one can receive an offer without the other. Stipends of up to ₹1 lakh a month are paid per intern, never split.
2. **The brief is open:** There's no set problem statement. Build any working application on Gnani AI's models or APIs.
3. **Your project uses Gnani AI technology:** Your submission names the Gnani AI models or APIs it uses and each member's contribution.
4. **You demo it in public:** Post your project on LinkedIn or X with the hashtags `#GnaniAI` and `#GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge`, then nominate those posts in your submission. The post has to be live before you submit.
5. **The deadline is fixed:** Final submissions close at 11:59 PM IST on November 10, 2026. You can edit project details until you submit.
6. **Keep it authentic:** Bought engagement, bots, paid promotion, engagement swaps, plagiarism and misleading demos aren't allowed. Entries with substantiated breaches may be disqualified.

## Selection process
1. **Engagement check:** Gnani AI verifies reactions, comments and reshares on nominated posts.
2. **Eligibility check:** Entries must show genuine use of Gnani AI technology and meet participation requirements.
3. **Shortlisting:** Strongest verified engagement entries are shortlisted for interview.
4. **Interviews:** Every shortlisted person interviews on their own.
5. **Written offers:** Based on candidate skills, interview performance, and role fit.
